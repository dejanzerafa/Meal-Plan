FAQ

Common questions.

Is there a free plan? +

Yes. The free tier gives you access to 14 full recipes with verified macros — no credit card needed. Upgrade to unlock the full library of 160+ recipes.

How are macros verified? +

Every recipe is calculated using a verified ingredient database with the 4/4/9 formula (protein × 4 + carbs × 4 + fat × 9 = kcal). All values are cross-checked — no estimates.

Does it work on iPhone and Android? +

SoulGainz is a PWA — it installs like a native app on any device. On iOS: Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android: tap Install when prompted. No App Store required.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Monthly plans cancel anytime, no questions asked. Every plan includes a 14-day money-back guarantee.

Do you have vegan recipes? +

Yes — there's a dedicated Vegan section with high-protein plant-based recipes. All recipes are oil-free and use natural fat sources.
