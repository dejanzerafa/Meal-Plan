FAQ

Questions

What's included in the free tier?
3 main course recipes (Marry Me Chicken, Beef Mince Bake, Buffalo Ranch Pasta), 1 breakfast recipe, and 1 dessert. Fully usable — shopping list, macros, cooking checklist — all included.
Can I use this offline?
Yes. Add the app to your iPhone home screen and it works completely offline — great for cooking in the kitchen without a phone signal.
Are the macros accurate?
Every recipe is calculated bottom-up from raw ingredient weights using USDA nutritional data. Not from packaging labels or estimates. As accurate as it gets without a lab.
How often are new recipes added?
Subscribers get new recipe drops every month. The library grows continuously — your subscription always includes the full library.
What if I buy once and you add more recipes?
Lifetime access owners get all recipes that exist at the time of purchase. New monthly drops are included with subscriptions — that's the value difference.
