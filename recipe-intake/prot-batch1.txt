bf66  🌾 Honey Nut Quinoa Breakfast Bowl
category: breakfast | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 284 kcal · 8.5 g protein · 46.1 C · 8.2 F  =  12% protein
target for breakfast: at least 30% (category floor), ideally 35% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Quinoa: 42.5 g
  - Almond milk: 120.0 g
  - Honey: 10.5 g
  - Chopped almonds: 4.4 g
  - Walnuts: 3.8 g
  - Cinnamon: 0.3 g
  - Banana: 29.5 g
METHOD:
  1. Rinse quinoa under cold water. Combine with almond milk in a saucepan and bring to a boil.
  2. Reduce heat to low heat, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes until fluffy.
  3. Stir in honey and cinnamon, then remove from heat.
  4. Divide between bowls and top with almonds, walnuts, and banana slices.
  5. Serve warm with an extra drizzle of honey if desired.
