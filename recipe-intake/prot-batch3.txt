bf61  🌾 Banana-Oat-Pancakes
category: breakfast | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 238 kcal · 11.6 g protein · 27.7 C · 9.6 F  =  19% protein
target for breakfast: at least 30% (category floor), ideally 35% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Banana: 59.0 g
  - Eggs: 60.0 g
  - Rolled oats: 20.0 g
  - Cinnamon: 0.3 g
  - Baking powder: 1.1 g
  - Coconut oil: 2.3 g
METHOD:
  1. Place banana, eggs, oats, cinnamon, and baking powder in a blender. Blend until smooth and slightly thick.
  2. Warm coconut oil in a nonstick skillet over 175°C.
  3. Pour about 60 ml of batter per pancake and cook for 2–3 minutes, or until bubbles form on the surface.
  4. Flip carefully and cook the other side for another 1–2 minutes until golden brown.
  5. Stack pancakes on a plate and top with your choice of berries, almond butter, or a drizzle of honey.

bf41  🥥 Bounty Overnight Weetabix
category: breakfast | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 523 kcal · 29.2 g protein · 60.4 C · 17.2 F  =  22% protein
target for breakfast: at least 30% (category floor), ideally 35% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Weetabix: 37.6 g
  - Coconut protein yogurt: 200.0 g
  - Unsweetened almond milk: 55.0 g
  - Greek yogurt (0% fat): 25.0 g
  - Desiccated coconut (unsweetened): 10.0 g
  - Dark chocolate 85%: 10.0 g
  - Banana (sliced): 60.0 g
METHOD:
  1. Crush the Weetabix into a container, pour over 50 ml of the almond milk and mix until fully combined. Press everything down firmly to create the base.
  2. Add the coconut protein yogurt and sprinkle over the desiccated coconut.
  3. Melt the dark chocolate, then stir it together with the Greek yogurt and the remaining 5 ml of almond milk until smooth.
  4. Spread the chocolate mixture evenly over the coconut layer.
  5. Top with the sliced banana, lid on, and refrigerate overnight — or at least 4 hours.
  6. Crack through the chocolate top and eat cold.

  - 0% Greek yogurt: 120.0 g
  - Blueberries: 120.0 g
  - Granola (low-sugar): 35.0 g
  - Honey: 7.0 g
  - Vanilla extract: 2.0 g
  - Maple syrup: 5.0 g
  - Lemon (juice): 5.0 g
