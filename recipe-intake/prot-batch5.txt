ds7  🍫 Frozen Banana Snickers Bar
category: dessert | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 284 kcal · 7.5 g protein · 24.3 C · 19.3 F  =  11% protein
target for dessert: at least 23% (category floor), ideally 33% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Banana (halved lengthways): 60.0 g
  - Peanut butter (natural): 14.0 g
  - Roasted peanuts (chopped): 10.0 g
  - Dark chocolate 85% (melted): 12.0 g
  - Coconut oil: 2.0 g
METHOD:
  1. Halve the banana lengthways — flat pieces give you a stable base to layer everything onto.
  2. Freeze the banana for 10 minutes before dipping. A cold base sets the chocolate faster and far more neatly.
  3. Spread the peanut butter over the cut side. This is the caramel layer that makes it taste like the candy bar.
  4. Press the chopped peanuts into the peanut butter so they don't roll off when you dip.
  5. Melt the chocolate with the coconut oil and drizzle over — the oil helps it set into a clean, snappy shell.
  6. Add a pinch of flaky salt right after the chocolate, before the shell hardens.
  7. Freeze on parchment at least an hour until firm.

d6  🍎 Baked Apple & Blueberry Crumble
category: dessert | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 371 kcal · 15.6 g protein · 60 C · 10 F  =  17% protein
target for dessert: at least 23% (category floor), ideally 33% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Apples (diced): 150.0 g
  - Blueberries: 50.0 g
  - Rolled oats: 30.0 g
  - Vanilla whey protein: 10.0 g
  - Almond flour: 7.5 g
  - Butter (melted): 3.8 g
  - Maple syrup: 12.5 g
  - Cinnamon (ground): 1.0 g
  - Lemon juice: 1.3 g
  - Cornstarch: 0.7 g
METHOD:
  1. Heat the oven to 190°C.
  2. Toss the apples and blueberries with the cinnamon, lemon juice and cornstarch. The cornstarch is what stops the fruit juice flooding the dish and steaming the topping.
  3. Tip the fruit into a small oven-safe dish.
  4. Combine the oats, protein powder and almond flour in a bowl.
  5. Rub through the melted butter and maple syrup until the mixture clumps. Stop while it is still uneven — the loose and clumped bits together are what gives you crumble rather than a lid.
  6. Scatter over the fruit and bake 25–30 min until golden and bubbling at the edges.
  7. Cool before covering and refrigerating.

ds14  🫙 Apple Cinnamon Yogurt Parfait
category: dessert | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 220 kcal · 12.5 g protein · 31.3 C · 5.6 F  =  23% protein
target for dessert: at least 23% (category floor), ideally 33% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Greek yogurt: 122.0 g
  - Apple: 90.0 g
  - Honey: 10.5 g
  - Cinnamon: 0.3 g
  - Granola: 7.0 g
  - Chopped walnuts: 3.8 g
METHOD:
  1. In a bowl, stir together the yogurt, honey, and cinnamon until smooth and well combined.
  2. In serving glasses, layer a spoonful of the yogurt mixture, followed by diced apple, granola, and walnuts, spreading each layer evenly.
  3. Repeat the layers until the glasses are filled, finishing with a touch of yogurt and a few walnut pieces on top.
  4. Chill briefly if desired, then serve immediately for a creamy, crunchy, and sweet breakfast or snack.

m180  🥚 Quinoa-Stuffed Eggplant Boats
category: main | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 249 kcal · 7.3 g protein · 37.7 C · 9.4 F  =  12% protein
target for main: at least 21% (category floor), ideally 37% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Eggplant: 225.0 g
  - Cooked quinoa: 92.0 g
  - Olive oil: 7.0 g
  - Diced tomatoes: 60.0 g
  - Chopped spinach: 3.8 g
  - Garlic clove: 1.5 g
  - Oregano: 0.3 g
METHOD:
  1. Preheat oven to 190°C. Scoop out eggplant flesh and chop it finely.
  2. Sauté chopped eggplant and garlic in olive oil for 5 minutes.

  4. Spoon filling into eggplant shells and bake for 20 minutes.
