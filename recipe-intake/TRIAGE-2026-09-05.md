## KEEP — 5

### 1. Spinach Tagliatelle Bolognese · 4 servings
Fills the largest gap in the library. Zero bolognese across 214 recipes.

**Post claims:** 570 kcal · 44P · 65C · 15F
**Computed:** 532 kcal · **35P** · 72C · 11F

Protein overstated by 26%. 400 g of 5% beef split four ways is 100 g raw per
serving ≈ 25 g protein; the pasta adds ~11 g. There is no route to 44 g.
Build at the computed figures.

### 2. Protein Cherry Cheesecakes · 4 servings
Zero cheesecake in the library.

**Post claims:** 285 kcal · 27P · 31C · 6F
**Computed:** **474 kcal** · 28P · 47C · **19F**

Calories understated by 66%, fat by more than 3×. 300 g cream cheese + 20 g butter
+ 120 g graham crackers across only four servings cannot be 285 kcal — the base
alone is ~660 kcal. Protein is the only figure that survives.

Recommend rebuilding lighter rather than publishing at 474: drop cream cheese to
200 g, base to 80 g, which lands ~355 kcal · 27P · 36C · 12F and keeps the format.

### 3. Birthday Cake Protein Waffles · 4 servings
Zero waffles.

**Post claims:** 390 kcal · 32P · 48C · 8F
**Computed:** 354 kcal · 32P · **38C** · 7F

Carbs overstated by 25%. Protein and fat are right. Freezer-friendly, which suits
the batch-prep model well.

### 4. Baked Apple & Blueberry Crumble · 4 servings
Zero dessert crumble.

**Post claims:** 310 kcal · 13P · 49C · 8F
**Computed:** **388 kcal** · 14P · 59C · **12F**

Calories understated 20%, fat 50%. 25 g butter is doing more damage than the post
accounts for. Cutting butter to 15 g and leaning on the apple's own moisture gets
it to ~340 kcal without hurting the texture.

### 5. Bounty Overnight Weetabix · 1 serving
Only non-oat overnight base in the library. Authored at `portions: 1` already,
which matches our model exactly.

**Post claims:** 490 kcal · 31P · 58C · 13F
**Computed:** 523 kcal · 29P · 59C · **17F**

Fat understated ~35%, and it swings on which coconut protein yoghurt is used —
worth specifying the brand assumption in the recipe.

**Method bug:** the ingredient list says 50 ml milk, but step 1 uses 50 ml and
step 3 uses "the remaining 5 ml". Needs to be 55 ml in the list, or 45 ml in step 1.

whey, eggs, peanut butter, cinnamon, honey, baking powder. The submission is the

### 7. Big Breakfast Power Bowls — covered three times over
`bf5` (egg + beef + cheese, 7 portions), `bf1` (bacon + hashbrown + egg), and
`bf36` (crispy potato + turkey + egg, pending) between them cover beef, bacon,
egg and potato breakfast bowls.

Its macros are actually the most accurate in the batch — computed 580 kcal ·
53P · 34C · 24F against a claim of 552 · 51 · 39.6 · 20.4. But accuracy does not
create a gap.

**Worth salvaging:** the three Greek-yoghurt protein sauces (buffalo ranch,
chipotle southwest, honey mustard) are genuinely novel and reusable across
existing bowls. Recommend lifting those as a condiments entry and dropping
the bowl. Note the post's "6k onion" is a typo for 6 g.

### 8. Protein Ice Cream Prep — equipment dependency
Step 5 is "process using the machine's light-ice-cream setting" — this needs a
Ninja Creami. No recipe in the library assumes an appliance beyond an oven, hob,
blender and waffle iron. Publishing it means most users cannot make it and the
ones who try without the machine get an ice block.

Cut, or rewrite as a no-churn freezer bark, which is a different recipe.

### 9. Protein Carrot Cake — marginal, your call
`bf38 🥕 Carrot Cake Baked Oats` (pending) and `bf3 🥕 Carrot Protein Pancakes`
(live) already cover carrot cake as a flavour.

Counter-argument for keeping: a frosted 9-piece tray bake is a different eating
occasion from breakfast oats, and it would sit in desserts, not breakfast.
Counter-argument against: the library already has three brownie recipes and a
banana bread; another tray bake is thin ground.

**Post claims:** 235 kcal · 16P · 27C · 8F
**Computed:** 212 kcal · 17P · **21C** · 7F — carbs overstated 29%.
