sn29  🫘 Spicy Roasted Chickpeas
category: salad | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 248 kcal · 11.6 g protein · 38 C · 6.4 F  =  19% protein
target for salad: at least 35% (category floor), ideally 42% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Canned chickpeas: 164.0 g
  - Olive oil: 2.2 g
  - Paprika: 0.6 g
  - Cayenne pepper: 0.2 g
METHOD:
  1. Preheat your oven to 200°C, giving it enough time to fully heat so the chickpeas crisp properly.
  2. Pat the chickpeas dry with a clean towel, then toss them in a bowl with the olive oil, paprika, cayenne, and salt, making sure every chickpea is evenly coated.
  3. Spread them out on a baking sheet in a single layer, giving them space so they roast instead of steam.
  4. Roast for 25–30 minutes, shaking the pan halfway through to help them crisp on all sides.
  5. Let them cool completely on the baking sheet; they firm up as they cool, giving you that perfect crunch.
