bf70  🌾 Strawberry Almond Baked Oatmeal Cups
category: breakfast | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 156 kcal · 5.3 g protein · 27 C · 3.4 F  =  14% protein
target for breakfast: at least 30% (category floor), ideally 35% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Rolled oats: 20.0 g
  - Almond milk: 40.0 g
  - Egg: 10.0 g
  - Honey: 14.0 g
  - Diced strawberries: 12.5 g
  - Baking powder: 0.8 g
  - Vanilla extract: 0.7 g
  - Sliced almonds: 1.5 g
METHOD:
  1. Preheat oven to 175°C.
  2. In a bowl, mix the oats and baking powder, then stir in the almond milk, egg, honey and vanilla extract.

target for breakfast: at least 30% (category floor), ideally 35% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Almond flour: 18.0 g
  - Rolled oats: 5.0 g
  - Eggs: 15.0 g
  - Honey: 10.5 g
  - Almond milk: 7.5 g
  - Fresh blueberries: 9.5 g
  - Baking powder: 0.6 g
  - Vanilla extract: 0.5 g
METHOD:
  1. Preheat oven to 175°C and line a muffin tin with paper liners.
  2. In a mixing bowl, whisk eggs, honey, almond milk, and vanilla until combined.
  3. Add almond flour, oats, and baking powder; mix into a thick batter.
  4. Gently fold in blueberries.
  5. Spoon the batter into muffin cups, filling each about ¾ full.

  - Natural peanut butter: 16.0 g
  - Banana: 59.0 g
  - Chia seeds: 5.5 g
  - Cinnamon: 0.7 g

  1. In a jar, stir together the rolled oats, almond milk, peanut butter, half the mashed banana, the chia seeds and the cinnamon until evenly combined.

bf57  🥞 Lemon Ricotta Pancakes
category: breakfast | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 287 kcal · 13.5 g protein · 37.8 C · 8.5 F  =  19% protein
target for breakfast: at least 30% (category floor), ideally 35% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Whole-wheat flour: 31.0 g
  - Baking powder: 1.1 g
  - Honey: 10.5 g
  - Low-fat ricotta cheese: 62.0 g
  - Egg: 30.0 g
  - Almond milk: 60.0 g
  - Lemon zest: 2.5 g
  - Vanilla extract: 1.1 g
METHOD:
  1. In a bowl, whisk ricotta, egg, almond milk, honey, lemon zest, and vanilla until smooth.
  2. In another bowl, mix flour and baking powder.
  3. Combine wet and dry ingredients; stir gently.
  4. Heat a lightly greased non-stick skillet to 175°C and pour 60 ml batter per pancake.
  5. Cook each side for 2–3 minutes until golden.
  6. Serve with a drizzle of honey and extra lemon zest for brightness.
