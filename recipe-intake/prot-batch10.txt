ds16  🌱 Crispy Air-Fried Tofu Bites
category: salad | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 88 kcal · 7.3 g protein · 2 C · 6.4 F  =  33% protein
target for salad: at least 35% (category floor), ideally 42% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Firm tofu: 88.0 g
  - Olive oil: 2.2 g
  - Soy sauce: 1.3 g
  - Garlic powder: 0.4 g
METHOD:
  1. Add the tofu cubes to a bowl and drizzle them with the olive oil and soy sauce.
  2. Sprinkle the garlic powder over the top, then toss everything together until each cube is evenly coated and lightly seasoned.
  3. Arrange the tofu pieces in a single layer in your air fryer basket and cook at 190°C for 10–12 minutes, shaking the basket halfway through to help them crisp evenly on all sides.
  4. Serve the tofu hot and golden with your favorite dipping sauce for a cozy, satisfying snack.

category: smoothie | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 454 kcal · 28.7 g protein · 27 C · 27.8 F  =  25% protein
target for smoothie: at least 29% (category floor), ideally 38% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Pineapple chunks: 82.0 g
  - Coconut milk: 120.0 g
  - Banana: 29.5 g
  - Hemp seeds: 5.0 g
  - Vanilla extract: 1.1 g
  - Whey protein (vanilla) — our addition: 30.0 g
METHOD:
  1. Add pineapple and banana into the blender first to form a fruity base.
  2. Pour in coconut milk and add vanilla extract.

  5. Pour into glasses and enjoy immediately post-workout for natural recovery.
