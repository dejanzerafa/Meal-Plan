bf71  🍪 Apple Cinnamon Protein Muffins
category: breakfast | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 141 kcal · 8.9 g protein · 19 C · 3.5 F  =  25% protein
target for breakfast: at least 30% (category floor), ideally 35% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Oat flour: 15.0 g
  - Vanilla protein powder: 5.0 g
  - Baking powder: 0.8 g
  - Cinnamon: 0.2 g
  - Apple: 30.0 g
  - Eggs: 20.0 g
  - Unsweetened applesauce: 10.0 g
  - Honey or maple syrup: 3.5 g
METHOD:
  1. Preheat oven to 175°C and lightly grease a muffin tin.
  2. In a bowl, whisk together oat flour, protein powder, baking powder, and cinnamon.
  3. In another bowl, combine eggs, applesauce, grated apple, and honey.
  4. Stir wet ingredients into dry until just combined.
  5. Pour batter into muffin tin and bake 18–20 minutes, until golden and set.
  6. Cool for 5 minutes before serving. These muffins fill the air with warm cinnamon sweetness — perfect with morning tea.

bf50  🫙 Strawberry Chia Parfait
category: breakfast | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 253 kcal · 16.3 g protein · 23.1 C · 10.6 F  =  26% protein
target for breakfast: at least 30% (category floor), ideally 35% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Unsweetened almond milk: 240.0 g
  - Chia seeds: 21.0 g
  - Vanilla extract: 1.1 g
  - Greek yogurt: 122.0 g
  - Sliced strawberries: 37.5 g
  - Honey: 3.5 g
METHOD:
  1. In a mixing bowl, whisk together almond milk, chia seeds, and vanilla extract. Stir for about 30 seconds to evenly distribute the chia seeds.
  2. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours (or overnight) until the mixture becomes thick and pudding-like.
  3. Once set, stir the chia pudding well to break up any clumps.
  4. In serving glasses, layer chia pudding, Greek yogurt, and sliced strawberries.
  5. Drizzle each parfait with honey and serve chilled for a refreshing, protein-rich start to your morning.

ds18  🫐 Dark Chocolate-Dipped Strawberries
category: dessert | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 95 kcal · 1.3 g protein · 9.2 C · 6.1 F  =  5% protein
target for dessert: at least 23% (category floor), ideally 33% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Strawberries: 36.0 g
  - Dark chocolate: 14.0 g
METHOD:
  1. Melt the chocolate in a microwave-safe bowl, heating it in 15-second bursts and stirring between each round until it becomes smooth and glossy.
  2. Dip each strawberry halfway into the warm chocolate, allowing any excess to drip off, then place them carefully on a sheet of parchment paper.
  3. Chill the strawberries in the refrigerator for about 15 minutes, or until the chocolate sets and becomes firm with a lovely shine.

ds22  🍪 Spiced Almond Energy Bites
category: dessert | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 154 kcal · 3.5 g protein · 18.1 C · 8.8 F  =  9% protein
target for dessert: at least 23% (category floor), ideally 33% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Almonds: 14.0 g
  - Medjool dates: 19.0 g
  - Cinnamon: 0.3 g
  - Nutmeg: 0.1 g
  - Chia seeds: 1.1 g
  - Coconut oil: 1.4 g
  - Vanilla extract: 0.4 g
METHOD:
  1. In a food processor, combine almonds and dates; pulse until finely chopped.
  2. Add cinnamon, nutmeg, chia seeds, coconut oil, and vanilla. Blend until sticky dough forms.
  3. Roll into bite-sized balls and refrigerate for 30 minutes before serving.
