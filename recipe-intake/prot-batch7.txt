m188  🫘 Chickpea Coconut Curry with Basmati Rice
category: main | portions: 1 | VEGETARIAN (no meat/fish anywhere) — dairy/plant protein ONLY
now: 824 kcal · 30.1 g protein · 126.2 C · 24.9 F  =  15% protein
target for main: at least 21% (category floor), ideally 37% (live median)
INGREDIENTS (per portion):
  - Coconut oil: 7.0 g
  - Onion: 75.0 g
  - Garlic cloves: 3.0 g
  - Curry powder: 3.1 g
  - Turmeric: 0.8 g
  - Chickpeas (canned, drained): 335.0 g
  - Light coconut milk: 120.0 g
  - Diced tomatoes: 120.0 g
  - Cooked basmati rice: 98.0 g
METHOD:
  1. Heat coconut oil in a skillet over 175°C and sauté onion until golden.
  2. Add garlic, curry powder, and turmeric, stirring until aromatic (about 1 minute).
  3. Add chickpeas, coconut milk, and tomatoes. Simmer for 10–12 minutes until thickened.
  4. Serve hot over basmati rice and top with fresh cilantro.
