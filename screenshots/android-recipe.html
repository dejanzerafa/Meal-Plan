Chicken & Rice Power Bowl🟢 High protein
Per serving · Batch: 5 portions · 35 min prep
640
kcal
76g
protein
68g
carbs
12g
fat
Ingredients
Chicken breast250g
Basmati rice (dry)100g
Broccoli150g
Soy sauce (low sodium)15ml
Olive oil8ml
Steps
1
Cook basmati rice in 250ml water for 12 min. Rest 5 min covered.
2
Season chicken with salt and pepper. Sear in oil 6–7 min per side until cooked through.
3
Steam broccoli 4 min. Slice chicken, assemble bowls. Drizzle soy sauce.
