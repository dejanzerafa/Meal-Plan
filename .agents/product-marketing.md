# Product Marketing Context — SoulGainz

*Last updated: 2026-08-16*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** High-protein meal prep app with verified macros — batch cook 7 days of food in one session.

**What it does:** SoulGainz is a PWA (Progressive Web App) and native mobile app that helps fitness-focused people plan, track, and batch-cook high-protein meals every week. Users browse a recipe library (94+ recipes), assign meals to breakfast/lunch/dinner slots, and the app auto-generates a grocery list scaled to their batch size. A built-in macro calculator gives personalised daily targets based on body stats, goals, and activity level.

**Product category:** Meal prep app · High-protein diet tracker · Fitness nutrition tool

**Product type:** Consumer SaaS/PWA (web + iOS/Android)

**Business model:**
- Free tier: 14 recipes (enough for a full week trial)
- Monthly subscription: $16.99/mo → 53 recipes
- Annual subscription: $12.50/mo (billed $150/yr) → 173 recipes — saves 2 months vs monthly
- Single-recipe purchase: $1.99 each
- Seasonal/SoulFood bundle: explicit one-off purchase (holiday recipes)
- Wave2 library: 66 recipes held back — released in periodic content drops to create ongoing engagement

## Target Audience
**Target users:** Fitness-oriented individuals (gym-goers, bodybuilders, weight-loss focused, body recomposition) who are time-poor during the week but motivated on weekends to cook.

**Primary demographics:**
- Age 20–40, predominantly male but growing female segment
- Active gym members (3–6 sessions/week)
- Working professionals who want nutrition sorted without meal delivery cost
- People frustrated with tracking apps that don't also help them cook

**Primary use case:** Plan + batch-cook 7 days of high-protein meals in a single Sunday session.

**Jobs to be done:**
- "Hit my protein target every day without thinking about it"
- "Stop wasting money on meal delivery while eating clean"
- "Have food ready so I don't make bad choices when tired"

**Use cases:**
- Bulk/muscle-gain: high-kcal, high-protein weekly prep
- Fat loss/recomp: calorie-controlled, macro-precise meals
- Time-saving: one prep session feeds the whole week
- Budget eating: batch cooking vs meal kit services

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Eating high-protein consistently is hard when you're busy — either you order expensive food or you don't track anything and plateau.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- MyFitnessPal/Cronometer: track macros but don't help you plan or cook
- Meal delivery kits (HelloFresh, etc.): expensive, not macro-optimised, daily cooking
- Generic recipe apps: no macro data, no batch scaling, no grocery list
- Eating the same 3 meals forever: boring, leads to giving up

**What it costs them:** Stalled results despite training hard; money wasted on food that doesn't align with goals; mental load of "what do I eat today?"

**Emotional tension:** "I train consistently but my diet is a mess — I know that's the thing holding me back."

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Mealime, Prepear — meal planning apps. Fall short because they're not macro/fitness-focused; no calorie/protein targets; not built for batch prep.

**Secondary:** MyFitnessPal + cooking from scratch — falls short because tracking-only without meal ideas is too much cognitive overhead.

**Indirect:** Meal delivery services (HelloFresh, My Muscle Chef) — falls short because expensive ($12–18/meal), can't customise macros, not batch-prep friendly.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Every recipe has verified macros (not estimated) — protein/carbs/fat/kcal per portion
- Built for batch prep — grocery list auto-scales to 3, 5, or 7 days
- Macro calculator integrated — recipes scale to YOUR targets, not generic portions
- All categories covered: mains, breakfast, pre-workout, snacks, dessert, smoothies
- Growing recipe library with wave2 content drops — subscribers get new recipes over time
- Mobile-first PWA + native app (offline capable)

**How we do it differently:** SoulGainz is the only app that combines macro-precise recipes WITH a meal planner, macro calculator, AND auto-grocery list in one tool — designed specifically for the batch-cook workflow.

**Why customers choose us:** "I can plan my whole week in 5 minutes, get one shopping list, and cook everything in 2 hours. I've been eating clean for 3 months straight."

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I can just Google recipes" | These recipes have verified macros scaled to YOUR targets, and generate your grocery list automatically. Google can't do that. |
| "It's too expensive" | Less than 1 meal delivery per month. You'll spend less on food overall by batch cooking. |
| "I won't stick to it" | Free tier lets you try a full week first. No credit card needed. 30-day money-back guarantee. |

**Anti-persona:** Casual home cooks who don't track macros or have fitness goals. People who prefer variety over consistency (new recipe every night). Vegans/vegetarians (the library is heavily meat-focused).

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** "Spending $15/day on takeaway because I have nothing prepped. Tracking on MFP but can't find the right meals. My diet is inconsistent so I can't tell if my training is working."

**Pull:** "One shopping list. One cook session. Macros hit every day. I can actually see progress now."

**Habit:** "I already use [MFP/other app] and don't want to switch everything." → Frame as complementary, not replacement.

**Anxiety:** "What if I get bored of the recipes?" → Wave2 drops + 94+ recipes to start + seasonal bundles. "What if it's too complicated?" → Free trial with no card required.
