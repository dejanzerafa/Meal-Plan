Home Screen

No App Store needed. Get the full SoulGainz experience installed on your phone in under 30 seconds.

1
Open Safari on your iPhone
Make sure you're using Safari — not Chrome or another browser. The install option only appears in Safari on iOS.
Open Safari → go to soulgainz.app
2
Tap the Share button
At the bottom of Safari, tap the Share icon — it looks like a box with an arrow pointing up.
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the share sheet until you see "Add to Home Screen" — tap it.
4
Tap "Add" to confirm
A screen appears showing the app name and icon. Tap Add in the top-right corner.
5
Open SoulGainz from your home screen
You'll find the SoulGainz icon on your home screen. Tap it — it opens fullscreen, no browser bar, just like a native app. 🎉

Ready? Open soulgainz.app in Safari and follow the steps above.

Common questions

Is this a real app or just a website?
It's a Progressive Web App (PWA) — it installs and runs exactly like a native app, works offline, and gets updates automatically. No App Store required.
Will it work without internet?
Yes — once installed, the app and your recipes load offline. An internet connection is only needed to sign in or sync new content.
I don't see "Add to Home Screen" in Safari
Make sure you're using Safari (not Chrome or Firefox) on your iPhone. The option appears in the Share sheet — scroll down if you don't see it immediately.
Can I access it on my laptop too?
Yes — just go to soulgainz.app in any browser. Your account and progress sync across all your devices automatically.
