1. Overview

SoulGainz ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and how we use and protect it.

SoulGainz is operated by Dejan Zerafa, based in Qatar. By using the app at soulgainz.app, you consent to the practices described in this Policy.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. We do not use your data for advertising profiling or behavioural tracking.

5. Data Storage & Security

Where your data is stored

Account data (name, email, date of birth, payment status) is stored in our Supabase database, hosted on infrastructure in the United States (AWS). Your body metrics (height, weight, gender, activity level, goal) and the calorie and macronutrient targets derived from them are stored both on your device and in our Supabase database, so your plan is available when you sign in on another device. Recipe selections, notes, pantry contents and custom recipes are stored only on your device and are not sent to our servers — which also means they are not recoverable if you reinstall.

How we protect your data

  • All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Database access is protected by Row Level Security (RLS) — you can only access your own data
  • Admin API access is protected by a strong secret key, never exposed publicly
  • We do not store payment card information — all card processing is handled directly by Stripe
  • API keys and secrets are stored as environment variables, never in the application code or repository

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will permanently remove your personal data from our database within 30 days. Some data may be retained for longer periods where required by law (e.g., financial transaction records for tax purposes — Stripe retains these per their legal obligations).

7. Your Rights

SoulGainz is operated from Qatar and processes personal data in accordance with Qatar's Personal Data Privacy Protection Law (Law No. 13 of 2016). You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to be informed — know how and why your personal data is processed
  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate, incomplete or outdated data
  • Right to deletion — request permanent deletion of your account and data
  • Right to object — object to processing, including for direct marketing
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out
  • Right to opt out of marketing — unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time using the link in any email we send
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a machine-readable format

Health and body data you enter (weight, height, goals) is treated as data of a special nature and is processed only with your explicit consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time and we will delete it.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also hold these rights under the GDPR / UK GDPR, including the right to restrict processing and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@soulgainz.app. We will respond within 30 days.

Complaints. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to Qatar's National Cyber Governance and Assurance Affairs (NCGAA), part of the National Cyber Security Agency, at ncsa.gov.qa. EEA and UK users may instead complain to their own national data protection authority.

Data breaches. If a breach occurs that is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify the competent authority and affected users without undue delay and, where required, within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

8. Cookies & Local Storage

SoulGainz does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. We use browser localStorage to save your app preferences, selected recipes, macro settings, and onboarding state locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Stripe may set session cookies during the payment process. These are strictly necessary for completing your transaction and are governed by Stripe's privacy policy.

9. Children's Privacy

SoulGainz is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@soulgainz.app and we will promptly delete it.

10. International Users

If you access the service from outside Qatar (including from the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or elsewhere), please be aware that your data may be transferred to and processed in countries that may not have the same level of data protection as your home country.

By using SoulGainz, you consent to this transfer. We take reasonable steps to ensure your data remains protected in accordance with this Policy regardless of where it is processed.
