Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 May 2025 · Last updated: May 2025
🔒 The short version: almost all your data stays on your device. We collect very little. We sell nothing.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Meal Prep application ("the App") and its associated services. When we say "we", "us", or "our", we refer to the App operator.

2. What Data We Collect

We believe in collecting as little data as possible. Here's exactly what we collect and why:

Data Where stored Why Who sees it
Meal selections, notes, history Your device (localStorage) To remember your preferences Only you
Premium unlock status Your device (localStorage) To remember you've paid Only you
Payment information Stripe servers To process payment Stripe (PCI compliant)
Email address (if provided) Our servers / email provider Order confirmation, account access Us only
Basic analytics (if enabled) Analytics provider Understand how the app is used Anonymised, no personal data

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The App uses browser localStorage to store the following data locally on your device:

  • Your selected lunch and dinner recipes (mp_lunch, mp_dinner)
  • Your personal notes on recipes (mp_note_[id])
  • Your meal history (mp_history)
  • Last cooked timestamps (mp_last_[id])
  • Portion preference (mp_portions)
  • Premium unlock status (mp_unlocked)
  • UI preferences (banner dismissal, PWA prompt)

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to any server. Clearing your browser data will erase it.

4. Payment Data

We use Stripe to process payments. When you make a purchase:

  • Your payment card details are entered directly into Stripe's secure form — we never see or store your card number
  • Stripe processes and stores your payment information in accordance with PCI DSS standards
  • We receive only a transaction confirmation, your email address (if provided), and the amount paid

Stripe's Privacy Policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

5. Analytics

We may use anonymised, aggregated analytics to understand how the App is used (e.g. which features are most popular, page load performance). If we do:

  • No personally identifiable information is collected
  • IP addresses are anonymised
  • We do not use analytics data to build personal profiles
  • We do not share analytics data with third parties for advertising

6. Cookies

The App uses localStorage, not traditional cookies, for storing user preferences. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

If you access the App via our landing page, that page may use essential cookies for performance. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

7. How We Use Your Data

We use the limited data we collect to:

  • Process and confirm payments
  • Send purchase confirmation emails (if applicable)
  • Provide access to premium content you have paid for
  • Improve the App based on anonymised usage patterns
  • Respond to support requests

We never sell your data. We never share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

8. Data Retention

Device data: Stored indefinitely on your device until you clear your browser data or uninstall the PWA.

Payment records: Retained as required by financial regulations (typically 7 years) and as outlined in Stripe's data retention policy.

Email address: Retained until you request deletion.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We will respond within 30 days.

Note: data stored in your browser's localStorage can be deleted at any time by clearing your browser data — this is fully within your control.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

11. International Users

The App is accessible worldwide. If you are accessing the App from outside the country in which we operate, please be aware that your information may be transferred to and processed in a different jurisdiction.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, data deletion requests, or to exercise your rights, please contact us through the App or via the contact information on our website. We aim to respond to all privacy requests within 30 days.
