Our Story

Built because we had the same problem.

We were training hard, tracking macros, and still ending up at 8pm with nothing prepped, no plan, and ordering something that undid the day's work.

Three apps open at once. Recipes on Google. Shopping list in Notes. Macros guessed from memory. It was chaos.

So we built SoulGainz — a single tool that gives you verified recipes, builds your grocery list automatically, and gets you through one batch cook session that covers the whole week.

170+ recipes and growing. Macros verified to the gram. One cook session. Seven days sorted.

Questions

Can I really cancel anytime? ›
Yes. Monthly and Annual plans can be cancelled at any time from your account page. Monthly cancellations take effect at the end of the billing period. Annual plans do not include pro-rated refunds.
How do I access the app after subscribing? ›
After subscribing, return to soulgainz.app and sign in with the same email you used to subscribe. Your account will be upgraded immediately. You can also use the SoulGainz app on your phone — sign in with the same credentials.
What are the annual-exclusive recipes? ›
Annual subscribers get 11 bonus recipes that aren't available on the monthly plan — premium dishes built for serious meal preppers who want maximum variety. These are included at no extra cost with your Annual subscription.
Is payment secure? ›
All payments are processed by Stripe — the same payment infrastructure used by Amazon, Shopify, and thousands of other businesses worldwide. We never store your card details.
Can I switch from Monthly to Annual? ›
Yes. You can upgrade to Annual at any time from your account settings. The remaining value of your Monthly subscription will be credited toward the Annual plan.
Do you offer refunds? ›
Monthly plans: no refunds (you keep full access until the period ends). Annual plans: contact us within 7 days of purchase for a refund at hello@soulgainz.app.
