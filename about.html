The Problem

Three apps open. Macros guessed. Food ordered. Again.

We were doing everything right at the gym. Progressive overload, enough sleep, protein targets dialled in — on paper. But then weekday reality hit: back from the gym at 7pm, fridge half-empty, and the usual debate about whether a chicken wrap from the petrol station counted as meal prep.

The information existed. There were thousands of high-protein recipes scattered across apps, blogs, YouTube, and PDF downloads. But stitching them into a plan, figuring out the macros, writing a shopping list, and actually cooking it all — that was a different project entirely.

"We weren't failing at the gym. We were failing at the kitchen — and it was undoing everything."

So we built SoulGainz to close that gap. Not another recipe database. A system — from recipe to grocery list to batch cook, all in one place, with macros you can actually trust.

How It Works

One session. Seven days sorted.

01
Browse & build your week
Choose from our growing recipe library sorted by protein, calories, prep time, and cuisine. Build your weekly plan in minutes — the app handles portion counts automatically.
02
Auto grocery list
Every ingredient, every quantity, combined and consolidated for you. One list. One shop. No mental maths, no missed items, no second trips.
03
Batch cook once
Follow the in-app cooking guide and prep your entire week in one session — typically 2–3 hours on a Sunday. Open fridge. Eat. Repeat.
04
Hit your macros every day
Every recipe has macros verified to the gram — not estimated, not approximated. What you see is what you eat. No more guessing, no more logging meals with a question mark.
