# Go-live audit — 2026-09-05

Two independent audits, findings verified by hand before being written here.
Every item cites file:line. Nothing in the older checklists was trusted.

**Verdict: not launchable today. Three to five focused days to a defensible launch.**

The engineering underneath is better than the surface suggests — the webhook
handles retries, out-of-order events and PostgREST's zero-row-200 correctly, the
rate limiter is real, the Apple-compliance separation is sound. What is missing
is the last mile, in the two places that matter most: **money and the front door.**

---

## What "going live" actually consists of

There is no App Store submission. The PWA is already deployed. Launch is:

1. Fix the blockers below.
2. Swap the two Stripe price IDs to live (`pricing.html:665`, `sign-up.html:259`),
   set `STRIPE_PRICE_MONTHLY` / `STRIPE_PRICE_ANNUAL` / `ALLOWED_PRICE_IDS` in
   Netlify, set the GitHub variable `STRIPE_MODE=live`.
3. Switch `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` and `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` in Netlify to the live
   key and the live webhook endpoint's secret (the live endpoint must be created
   in the Stripe dashboard pointing at `soulgainz.app/.netlify/functions/stripe-webhook`
   and subscribed to `checkout.session.completed`, `invoice.payment_succeeded`,
   `invoice.payment_failed`, `customer.subscription.updated`,
   `customer.subscription.deleted`).
4. Run the "front door" swap: `/` stops redirecting to the waitlist, the
   "Not yet — we're in final development" FAQ copy goes, the in-app "Join the
   Waitlist" CTA goes.
5. Send the launch email (drafted at `LAUNCH_EMAIL.md`) to the waitlist.
6. Instagram bio → `soulgainz.app`. Only after step 4.

Steps 2–3 are the "48 hours before" swap you have already planned. Step 1 is
what this document is about.

## BLOCKERS — cannot take a real payment until these are fixed

### B1. A paying customer lands on the app signed out and locked
`success.html:186-187` says "You're in! Your subscription is active" unconditionally.
It verifies nothing — `verify-session.js` exists and has zero callers.

The buyer pays on `marketing.soulgainz.app`, is sent to `soulgainz.app/success`.
Supabase stores the session in localStorage, which is per-origin
(`index.html:10269`, `marketing-site/pricing.html:671` — no shared cookie domain).
**Different origin, different localStorage. They arrive anonymous.** They click
"Open SoulGainz →", see the free tier and a "Join the Waitlist" button.

The in-app upgrade path works (a 90 s poller catches the new tier) but only for
someone already signed in *inside the app*. The marketing-first path — the one
every ad click takes — has no handoff.

The "Restore purchase" button in the ME tab is correctly wired and would fix
them, but only renders after they sign in again, and nothing tells them to.

**Fix:** `success.html` must call `verify-session`, and either share the session
via a cookie on `.soulgainz.app` or hand the token across and say "sign in with
the email you paid with". ~3 h.

### B2. Users are told purchases are non-refundable. The Terms promise 14 days.
`index.html:11999` and `:13055`: "All purchases are final and non-refundable."
`terms.html:186`: 14-day money-back guarantee on all plans.

`:13055` is inside the signup modal, next to the "I agree to the Terms" checkbox.
The customer-favourable version governs, so you are bound to refund — but every
user has been told they can't ask. Misleading-terms exposure.

`check-legal-consistency.mjs` missed it because its pattern requires a digit.
**Fix:** 30 min including a guard rule.

### B3. Fabricated five-star testimonials, one under your own name
`landing.html:2104-2151`, headed "Real results, real people", three ★★★★★
reviews on a product with no customers. The first is "Dejan, Gym-goer, Dublin".
Highest public-embarrassment item in the repo, and a consumer-protection
exposure alongside. **Fix:** 15 min to delete.

### B4. The marketing site is publicly served from the app domain
`netlify.toml` has `publish = "."` and `.netlifyignore` does not exclude
`marketing-site/`. **`soulgainz.app/marketing-site/pricing.html` serves a full
pricing page with €16.99/€150 and live Subscribe buttons, on the app domain.**
That is the App Store rule, breached. Also `landing.html:46,53` publishes the
prices as JSON-LD on the app domain; the compliance guard can't see it because
the amount is bare with the currency in a separate field.
**Fix:** 15 min.

### B5. Email confirmation is a dead end
`index.html:12900` calls `signUp()` with no `emailRedirectTo` (zero occurrences
in the file). The confirmation link lands on `/`, which 301s to `landing.html`,
which has no Supabase client. The token cannot be consumed.

Worse: `:12908-12922` shows "You're in!" and sets `sg_onboarded` regardless, so
the user is silently anonymous forever and the signup screen never reappears.
This is the exact bug already diagnosed and fixed for password reset at
`:12950-12957` — the fix was never applied to sign-up. **Fix:** ~1 h.

### B6. Nobody ever receives a welcome email
`save-user.js:171` sends it unless `skip_email`. The only live caller,
`index.html:11553`, hardcodes `skip_email: true`. The signup handler never calls
`save-user` at all. The Resend audience never grows. **Fix:** ~1 h.

### B7. The front door still says the product doesn't exist
`netlify.toml:85-88` 301s `/` to the waitlist landing page, whose countdown
targets Sept 1 and now renders "Live now!" above a Join-the-waitlist button.
`marketing-site/pricing.html:488` answers "Is SoulGainz live yet?" with
"Not yet — we're in final development" *on the page with the Subscribe buttons*.
Same at `about.html:223`, `contact.html:177`, `index.html:519`. In-app,
`index.html:16528` shows "Join the Waitlist — Get notified at launch".
**Fix:** ~1 h. `scripts/launch-swap.sh` exists but greps for retired tiers.

### B8. Stripe is on TEST price IDs — you know this (task #50)
`marketing-site/pricing.html:665`, `sign-up.html:259`. Both Subscribe buttons
either 400 or provision free accounts depending on which key is live.
The live price objects are already created; only the swap remains.

## SUPABASE — the data layer, verified from the SQL files

The app grants access from `profiles.tier`. That path works. Everything below
is about the `subscriptions` ledger and the identity model around it, which
have been failing silently because nothing user-facing reads them.

**Run `supabase-diagnostic-2026-09-05.sql`** (read-only, SELECTs only, safe on
production) and paste back the last result. Several items below are "the SQL
files say X" and the live DB may have been hand-altered — the diagnostic settles it.

### D1. `subscriptions.at_risk` does not exist. Four webhook writes fail.
No `.sql` file creates it. `stripe-webhook.js:270, 325, 337, 484` all include it.
PostgREST rejects the whole UPDATE. The error at `:270` isn't even captured.
Net effect: `invoice.payment_succeeded` never updates the subscriptions row
(entitlement still extends — that's written to `profiles` separately, which is
why nobody noticed), and `downgradeUserToFree` never cancels subscription rows.

### D2. The status CHECK rejects what Stripe sends
`supabase-schema.sql:58`: `CHECK (status IN ('active','cancelled',…))` — two Ls.
The webhook writes Stripe's spelling: `sub.status` at `:270, 291, 365` and the
literal `"canceled"` at `:484`. **Every cancellation update fails the CHECK; the
row stays `active` forever.** Consequences: `send-new-drop.js:44` keeps emailing
cancelled customers, `renewal-reminder` can email them, `restore-account.js:196`
restores access to cancelled users.

### D3. `events` has two incompatible definitions; analytics is probably dead
`supabase-schema.sql:107` (`event_type`, `user_id`, `metadata`) vs
`analytics.sql:2` (`event_name`, `session_id`, `email`, `properties`). Both
`IF NOT EXISTS` — whichever ran first won. `track-event.js` writes the second
shape, the webhook writes the first. One of them has been 400ing on every call,
swallowed. Given run order, most likely every analytics event since May.

### D4. Three identity spaces joined only by email string
`profiles.id` = auth uid. `users.id` = random uuid (`supabase-schema.sql:11`).
Nothing links them. `profiles.email` is **client-writable** (part-3 grant) and
is the join key the webhook uses for entitlement writes (`:161, :453, :496`),
with no uniqueness. A user can set their `profiles.email` to another customer's
address and receive their tier on the next webhook write. Fix belongs in SQL:
drop `email` from the authenticated UPDATE grant, unique index on `lower(email)`.

### D5. `promo_codes.redeemed_by` / `redeemed_at` probably don't exist
Only defined in `migrations/20260818`, whose `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` was a
no-op after `supabase-setup.sql`. `redeem-promo.js:137` PATCHes them. If promo
redemption works in production, someone added the columns by hand and the repo
cannot rebuild the database.

### D6. No account deletion exists anywhere
`privacy.html §7` promises deletion within 30 days. There is no
`auth.admin.deleteUser`, no client path, nothing. `promo_codes.created_by` and
`email_signups.user_id` are `NO ACTION` FKs, so deleting your own admin account
fails while any promo code exists. The whole `users` family has no FK to
`auth.users` at all — orphans on every deletion.

### D7. Consent default is wrong on Stripe-created rows
`users.marketing_opt_in` defaults `true` (`schema.sql:17`). `save-user.js:66`
now defaults `false` — but the webhook upsert at `:73` never sets it, so every
customer created via Stripe inherits `true` and gets holiday/birthday mail.

### D8. Duplication and dead weight
- Four tables hold email addresses: `users`, `waitlist`, `email_signups` (no
  writer anywhere — dead), `calc_email_sends`. Two different things are called
  "the waitlist" in code.
- `users.date_of_birth` is never written; birthday emails and `idx_users_dob`
  are dead. DOB lives in `profiles.dob` as text.
- `redemptions`, `recipe_unlocks`, `email_signups`, `profiles.is_admin`,
  `profiles.terms_accepted_at` — never written.
- Five redundant indexes duplicating UNIQUE constraints.
- `supabase/functions/*` Edge functions duplicate three Netlify functions.
- `profiles.dob`, `profiles.tier_expires`, `meal_logs.date` are `text`.
  `tier_expires` holds a full timestamp from Stripe and a bare date from promos
  in the same column (patched around at `index.html:14818`).

## SHOULD-FIX before the first customer

- **S1. Nothing alerts on a failed provisioning.** No Sentry in any function.
  `stripe-webhook.js:171` writes "UNMATCHED PURCHASE — manual grant required"
  to a log nobody watches. You find out from the complaint. ~3 h.
- **S2. Analytics consent absent; privacy policy is false about it.** GA4 loads
  in `<head>` on every page including `privacy.html` itself, no banner. §8 says
  "no tracking cookies". §4 names Formspree, which receives nothing; omits GA,
  Sentry and Google Fonts, which do. ~4 h.
- **S3. Dev-tier backdoor ships in the bundle.** `index.html:10223-10265` — two
  localStorage keys grant the full library and fake `_serverVerified`. PIN is an
  unsalted SHA-256 in the source. Not a real entitlement, but a revenue leak that
  will be found. ~2 h to build-flag out.
- **S4. Emails a customer expects and never gets:** renewal reminder (no
  scheduler), cancellation confirmation (fires weeks late), refund confirmation
  (no `charge.refunded` case). `cron-jobs.sql` ships with
  `REPLACE_WITH_CRON_SECRET_FROM_SUPABASE_VAULT` literal in all three jobs.
  **A live CRON_SECRET is in git history** — rotation is already scheduled.
- **S5. Terms acceptance not recorded on the path that leads to payment.**
  `marketing-site/pricing.html:929` calls `startCheckout` immediately after
  `signUp`, no checkbox, no record. ~2 h.
- **S6. No admin surface.** Seeing signups, fixing a stuck account, refunding —
  all curl with `ADMIN_SECRET`, or the Stripe dashboard by hand.
- **S7. `_mobile/www/index.html`** is an untracked Aug-20 snapshot carrying
  live price IDs and in-app checkout. Delete it.
