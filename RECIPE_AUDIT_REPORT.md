## Science Foundation: Key Thresholds Used in This Audit

### Protein Quality Tiers (app's `proteinQuality()` function)
| Dot | Threshold | Basis |
|-----|-----------|-------|
| 🟢 Green | ≥ 30% of kcal from protein | Supported by 2024 meta-analysis (47 studies, 3,218 participants) — optimal for fat loss whilst preserving muscle |
| 🟡 Amber | 20–29% of kcal | Moderate; acceptable for carb-forward or mixed meals |
| 🔴 Red | < 20% of kcal | Below fitness standard; needs justification |

### Protein Per Meal Targets
- **30g minimum** to trigger Leucine threshold (2.5g leucine) for muscle protein synthesis (MPS)
- **50g+ ideal** for main meals in a body composition context
- **≥ 40g** for breakfast to prevent muscle catabolism and improve satiety through the morning

### Daily Protein Target
- **1.6–2.4 g/kg body weight/day** (upper end during calorie deficits)
- Distribution: 3–4 protein feedings per day, each hitting the leucine threshold

### Carbohydrate Standards
- **Fibre target:** 25g/day (women), 38g/day (men)
- **Carb timing:** Pre/post training is beneficial; rest-day carbs can be reduced 15–25%
- **Best sources for meal prep:** Oats (GI 42), lentils (GI 32), chickpeas (GI 28), sweet potato (GI 44–54), quinoa (GI 53), brown rice (GI 50–55)
- **Avoid:** Jasmine rice over basmati; ultra-processed carb sources (Lotus/Biscoff, processed cereals)

### Fat Quality Standards
- **Omega-3:6 ratio target:** 4:1 or lower (Western diet averages 15:1 — a major risk factor)
- **Saturated fat limit:** < 10% of total kcal (< 22g/day at 2,000 kcal)
- **Healthy fats to prioritise:** Salmon, sardines, olive oil, avocado (in controlled portions), nuts
- **Avoid:** Palm oil, partially hydrogenated oils, processed meats high in saturated fat

### Food Quality (NOVA Framework)
- Target **NOVA Groups 1–2** (unprocessed or minimally processed)
- **Avoid Group 4:** Biscoff/Lotus spread, pepperoni, processed sausages, packet flavouring mixes
- 39 meta-analyses show zero beneficial health outcomes associated with ultra-processed food consumption

### Cooking Method (Nutrient Retention)
- **Steaming:** Retains 80–90% of water-soluble vitamins
- **Microwaving:** Comparable to steaming (≥ 80% retention)
- **Boiling:** Up to 50% vitamin C and B-vitamin loss
- **Broccoli:** Best lightly steamed (3–5 min) — preserves myrosinase enzyme for sulforaphane production

## Rating System

- ✅ **Good** — fitness-aligned, no significant issues, keep as-is
- ⚠️ **Needs improvement** — fixable with ingredient/proportion adjustments
- ❌ **Weak/Problematic** — fundamental macro issues, misleading naming, or poor ingredient quality

## CROSS-CATEGORY FINDINGS

### Breakfast is the biggest problem area
11 of 35 breakfasts deliver < 20% protein from kcal. Several carry misleading "power/protein" names. The smoothie bowl variants (bf18, bf19, bf22) are essentially fruit sugar bowls. The app would benefit from:
- A "Protein Focus" filter in the breakfast tab highlighting ≥30% protein recipes
- Clear labelling of "treat breakfast" vs "performance breakfast"

### Vegetarian mains consistently underperform
The entire v-series ranges 21–27% protein from kcal. Without meat, these recipes need mandatory protein amplification:
- Greek yogurt or cottage cheese stirred through every veg main
- Tofu always specified as "extra-firm" or "pressed"
- Hemp seeds, tempeh, or edamame added for protein stacking

### Smoothie category splits cleanly
sm4 and sm5 (berry-based) are genuinely excellent. sm1, sm3, sm7 are calorie-dense carb shakes — reformulate or flag as "mass gain" options.

### Holiday section is a hidden gem
hol1, hol2 are top 10 recipes in the app. hol3, hol5 are the worst holiday entries (lamb + rich preparations = poor macro profile).

### Dessert naming is the most dishonest
"High-Protein Chocolate Brownie" at 18.9%, "Mango Protein Tart" at 13.3%, "Protein Banana Bread" at 21.6% — all should earn their names or drop the claim.

### Ultra-processed ingredient alerts
Remove or replace in these recipes:
- **Lotus/Biscoff spread** (bf15): palm oil, refined sugar, NOVA Group 4
- **Pepperoni** (m23): processed meat, high saturated fat, high sodium
