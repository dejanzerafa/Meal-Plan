Questions answered

Is SoulGainz live yet? ▾
Not yet — we're in final development and launching soon. Join the waitlist to be first in the door. Waitlist members get early access before the public launch.
What's the difference between Monthly and Annual? ▾
Monthly gives you 85+ recipes, the unlimited macro calculator, the auto grocery list and weekly meal planning. Annual gives you all of that plus the full 160+ recipe library and early access to every new recipe we add. Annual works out at €12.50 a month against €16.99 — about 26% cheaper, or €54 saved over a year.
Can I get a refund if it's not for me? ▾
Yes. Every plan comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee. If SoulGainz isn't right for you, email support@soulgainz.app within 14 days of your first payment and we'll refund it in full — no forms, no interrogation. This doesn't affect any statutory rights you have where you live.
Can I cancel my subscription? ▾
Any time, in two taps — ME tab → Manage Account, or email support@soulgainz.app. Cancelling stops the next payment and you keep full access until the end of the period you've already paid for. We don't refund part-used periods outside the 14-day guarantee, and we never charge a cancellation fee.
Does my subscription renew automatically? ▾
Yes — monthly plans renew each month, annual plans each year, so your access never lapses unexpectedly. You can cancel before any renewal date. If we ever change the price, you'll get at least 30 days' notice by email and the new price only applies from your next renewal — so you always have time to cancel first.
What happens if a payment fails? ▾
Nothing dramatic. We retry the payment a few times, and if it still doesn't go through your account simply drops back to the free tier — your data, saved plans and settings all stay exactly where they are. Update your card and your full access comes straight back.
How does the macro calculator work on the free plan? ▾
The free plan includes one macro calculation, so you can get your personalised daily targets without paying anything. Any paid plan — monthly or annual — makes it unlimited, so you can recalculate whenever your weight, activity level or goal changes. Your saved targets stay usable either way; the limit only applies to running a new calculation.
Do I need to download anything? ▾
No App Store required. SoulGainz is a Progressive Web App — visit the site, tap "Add to Home Screen", and it installs like a native app. Works offline, loads instantly, and updates itself.
Are the macros actually accurate? ▾
Yes. Every recipe has been independently macro-verified, not estimated. The numbers you see are the numbers you get — that's a core principle of how we build the library. SoulGainz is a food and nutrition tool, though, not medical advice; if you have a health condition, talk to a qualified professional before changing your diet.
What payment methods do you accept? ▾
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay — all processed securely by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and are never stored on our servers.
Can I share my account with someone else? ▾
Subscriptions are for one person — one account each, and credentials shouldn't be shared. Your meal plans, macro targets and saved recipes are all built around your own numbers, so a shared account gives both people worse results anyway.
What if you shut the service down? ▾
You'd get at least 60 days' written notice by email, the app stays fully working through that whole period so you can export anything you want to keep, and every active subscriber receives a pro-rata refund for the unused part of their billing period.

These answers summarise our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy in plain English. Where anything differs, the full Terms apply. Questions? support@soulgainz.app
