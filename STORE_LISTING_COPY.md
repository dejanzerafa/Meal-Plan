🛒 SMART SHOPPING LIST
Your shopping list is automatically generated from your meal plan — scaled to portions, sorted by category, and ready to screenshot or check off in-store.

## NOTES FOR SUBMISSION

- **App Store Connect**: You'll need a 1024×1024 PNG icon (no alpha) — saved as `icon-1024.png` in project root
- **Google Play Console**: Requires 512×512 PNG icon + 1024×500 feature graphic
- **Screenshots**: minimum 3 × 6.7" iPhone screenshots for App Store; 2 × phone screenshots for Google Play
- **Both stores**: Privacy policy URL is required before submission
