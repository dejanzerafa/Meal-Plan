```

**Caption:**

```
Your chicken didn't lose protein. It lost water. 🍗

Three things worth knowing:

1️⃣ Chicken breast loses roughly 25% of its weight during cooking. 200g raw comes out around 150g cooked. The protein didn't go anywhere — the water did.

2️⃣ Most nutrition databases list meat raw. If you weigh it cooked and log the raw entry, you're under-counting your calories — often by 25–30% on that item.

3️⃣ Rice and pasta go the other way. 75g of dry rice becomes about 200g cooked. Log the dry weight or you'll wildly over-count.

Rule: weigh and log raw, or use a cooked-specific entry. Pick one and stay consistent.

Every SoulGainz recipe is built from raw ingredient weights, so the macros hold up.

### Topic A — "Protein per meal beats protein per day"

BEATS
PROTEIN PER DAY
