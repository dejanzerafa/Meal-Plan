## POST 7 — Macro Tip
**Image:** `sg_post7_tip.png`
**Angle:** Educational content that positions SoulGainz as the authority. Pure value, subtle CTA.

**Caption:**

Protein first. Always. 💪

Here's why most people don't lose fat or build muscle as fast as they should:

They're eating 80g of protein and calling it a day.

Science is clear — protein is the most important macro for:
✅ Muscle preservation in a deficit
✅ Satiety (staying full longer)
✅ Thermogenic effect (burns more calories to digest)

Target: 0.8–1g per pound of bodyweight. Every. Single. Day.

At 80kg (176lbs) that's ~141–176g daily. Most people aren't close.

SoulGainz was built around hitting that number. 86% of our recipes land at 30g+ protein per serving. Our macro calc gives you your actual personalised target — not a generic one.

Food should do the work. You just have to eat it. 🍱
