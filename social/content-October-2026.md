# 4. EDUCATION TOPICS — October

### Topic A — "Your cooking oil is 120 calories you didn't log"
**Image headline:** `YOUR COOKING OIL IS 120 CALORIES YOU DIDN'T LOG.`

Key facts for the caption:
1. One tablespoon (15ml) of olive oil ≈ 14g fat, ~120 kcal. Free-pouring typically delivers 1.5–2× that. Across a week of cooking that's 1,000–1,500 untracked kcal — enough to erase a fat-loss deficit.
2. "0 kcal" spray oil labels rely on a serving size of a fraction of a second. A realistic 2–3 second spray is 20–30 kcal. Not huge, but not zero, and it adds up over 14 meals.
3. Fat is 9 kcal/g vs 4 kcal/g for protein and carbs, so measurement error in fat is more than twice as expensive. Weigh or measure oil in ml — SoulGainz recipes specify it exactly and include it in the macros.

### Topic B — "Carbs aren't optional if you train"
**Image headline:** `CARBS AREN'T OPTIONAL IF YOU TRAIN.`

Key facts for the caption:
1. Muscle glycogen is the main fuel for high-intensity resistance work. Low glycogen means fewer reps at a given load and less total training volume — which is the stimulus you're chasing.
2. A meal with carbs and protein 2–3 hours pre-training improves session quality. Practical target: 1–1.5g carbs per kg bodyweight in that meal for a hard session (roughly 80–120g for an 80kg lifter).
3. For fat loss, total energy balance determines the result; carbs vs fat is a preference call within that. If you lift, keeping carbs and trimming fat protects performance — which is why every SoulGainz main pairs 40g+ protein with a real carb source.
