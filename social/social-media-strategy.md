## Monthly Recurring Content Batches

### Each month, produce the following:

**4 Recipe Posts**
- Feature a different recipe each week
- Always include: name, kcal, protein, carbs, fat, key ingredients
- Rotate protein sources: chicken / beef / turkey / fish / eggs

**2 Education Posts**
- Macro or nutrition tip
- Topics to rotate: protein timing, calorie deficit, meal frequency, carb cycling, fat sources, micronutrients, hydration, sleep and recovery

**2 Identity/Motivation Posts**
- Seasonal angles (cut season, bulk season, new year, summer)
- Always centred on the person, not the product

**1 BTS/Founder Post**
- App update, feature preview, or milestone (waitlist numbers, recipe count etc.)

**1 Social Proof Post (once launched)**
- User result, testimonial, or screenshot

**Total: ~10 feed posts per month + daily stories**

## Engagement Rules

1. **Reply to every comment** in the first hour after posting — this signals to Instagram the post is active
2. **Reply to every DM** — early followers become your most loyal advocates
3. **Use question boxes in Stories every 2–3 days** — responses tell you what content to make next
4. **Save any strong comments** — they become future post ideas or captions
5. **Never buy followers** — fake engagement tanks your reach with real people
6. **Cross-post your best Stories content to Close Friends** once you have 50+ followers — higher engagement rate
