## SECTION 1 — PROFILE AUDIT

### What the profile should communicate in 3 seconds

A first-time visitor lands on your profile. They have 3 seconds before they decide to follow or leave. In those 3 seconds they need to know:
1. What you do ("meal prep app")
2. Who it's for ("gym-goers, macro trackers")
3. Why you're different ("160+ recipes, verified macros, batch-optimised")

### Profile photo
**Recommendation:** Use the SoulGainz logomark (icon, not the wordmark). Should be the orange flame/icon on black background — no text, no gradient border. At 40px Instagram profile size, the wordmark is illegible. The icon alone reads instantly.

### Bio — Current issues
Instagram bios have 150 characters. Most brands waste them on vague mission statements.

**Bio to use:**
```
Cook once. Eat all week. 🍗
160+ high-protein meal prep recipes
Verified macros · Batch portions
👇 Free macro calculator
soulgainz.app
```
This hits: what you do, the proof point (160+), the differentiator (verified macros), a free hook (calculator), and the link.

### Link in bio
Use a single link to `soulgainz.app` or a Linktree with:
- "Try the free macro calculator"
- "Browse the recipe library"
- "Get full access — 160+ recipes"

### Highlights
Create 5 Highlight covers (dark background, orange icon):
1. 🍗 **Recipes** — best recipe reveal reels

3. 🥡 **Prep Day** — Sunday reset reels

## SECTION 2 — PROFILE DIAGNOSIS

### What's helping growth
- **"160+ recipes"** — a concrete, credible number. Use it in every caption.
- **Dark cinematic aesthetic** — stands out in a feed dominated by bright pastel food content.
- **Batch cooking angle** — underserved niche within fitness food. High save rate content.
- **MENA/Qatar angle** — geographic specificity builds authenticity and a community of regional followers.
- **Macro-verified claims** — trust builder. Competitors don't show actual numbers.

### What's hurting growth (common early-stage mistakes to avoid)
- **Inconsistency in posting cadence** — algorithm punishes gaps. Gaps of 4+ days reset reach.
- **Not using SEO in captions** — Instagram indexes caption keywords. Every caption should include "meal prep," "high protein," "batch cooking" for discoverability.
- **No clear bio CTA** — "link in bio" only works if the bio link is obvious and purposeful.
- **Missing alt text on posts** — Instagram allows alt text on images. Use it: "chipotle chicken bowl meal prep, 88g protein per serving." Helps with accessibility and search.
- **Not pinning best-performing Reels** — pin your 3 best posts. New visitors see your best work first.
- **No story consistency** — Stories are where followers become community. Without daily Stories, follower warmth drops fast.

### Biggest fixes (in order of impact)
1. Post every Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday, Sunday — no exceptions
2. Reply to every comment within the first hour of posting (boosts algorithm)
3. Pin top 3 Reels on profile
4. Post Stories daily — even just repurposing the day's Reel
5. Use audio from Instagram's trending sounds library every single time
6. Add location tag (Doha, Qatar) to every post — regional discovery is underused
7. Write alt text for every post

## SECTION 3 — BRAND VOICE GUIDE

### Tone
**Direct. No-fluff. Premium. Motivating.**

Think: the friend who's already done the work and is showing you the shortcut. Not preachy. Not salesy. Never condescending. Like someone who genuinely figured something out and wants to share it efficiently.

### The SoulGainz voice in 5 words
**Precise. Dark. Efficient. Real. Unapologetic.**

### Write like this
- Short sentences. One idea per line. White space.
- Numbers over adjectives: "89g protein" not "high protein"
- Present tense: "This is what Sunday looks like" not "This will change your Sundays"
- Direct second person: "You" not "people" or "everyone"
- Honest: "90 minutes" not "in no time"

### Never write like this
- "This incredible recipe will absolutely transform your fitness journey!"
- "We're so excited to share this amazing meal prep idea with you!"
- "As always, let us know in the comments!" (hollow filler)
- "Let's get cooking!" (food blogger energy)

### Words to use
cook, prep, batch, macros, protein, verified, sorted, efficient, system, habit, consistent, week, Sunday, portion, real, goal

### Words to avoid
amazing, incredible, delicious, yummy, obsessed, life-changing, hack, vibe, journey, healthy lifestyle, wellness

## SECTION 7 — POST PERFORMANCE FRAMEWORK

### What makes a SoulGainz post perform

**Hook** (first frame / first line) — 70% of success
- Specific number: "89g protein" beats "high protein"
- Creates a gap: "Most people don't know this about protein"
- Challenges: "You're probably 80g short every day"
- Curiosity: "Guess the macros on this"

**The first 3 seconds of every Reel determine if it gets pushed.**

Score your hooks before posting:
- Does it have a specific number? +2
- Does it create curiosity or a gap? +2
- Does it speak directly to a pain ("you're hungry", "results stalling")? +2
- Is it under 8 words? +1
- Does it avoid weak openers ("Today I'm sharing", "This is a quick", "Hey guys")? +1
- **Target: 7+/8**

### What kills performance
- Posting and ghosting (not replying to comments in first hour)
- Captions over 200 words with no line breaks (people don't read walls)
- No CTA (what should they do after watching?)
- Audio that isn't trending
- Thumbnail that doesn't show the food or the number

### Diagnosing weak posts
If a post underperforms (< 500 views in first 2 hours), check:
1. Hook — was it specific and curiosity-driving?
2. Audio — was it trending at the time of posting?
3. Retention — did the video lose people before the CTA?
4. Caption — was the first line weak?
5. Timing — did you post during a low-activity window?

### Post performance benchmarks (realistic for a growing account)
| Metric | Weak | Average | Strong |
|--------|------|---------|--------|
| Reach | <500 | 500–2k | 2k+ |
| Saves | <20 | 20–100 | 100+ |
| Comments | <5 | 5–30 | 30+ |
| Follows from post | <5 | 5–30 | 30+ |

**Saves are the most important metric.** High saves = Instagram pushes the post to more people. Every caption should be save-worthy ("save this for Sunday").

## SECTION 9 — ENGAGEMENT PLAYBOOK

### The reply rule
Reply to every comment within 60 minutes of posting. Every reply = more reach.

**How to reply well:**
- Don't just say "thanks!" — add value or extend the conversation
- If they comment a macro guess: give them the real number + "Want the full recipe? It's in the app"
- If they share a prep story: validate + ask a follow-up
- If they say they're starting meal prep: give one tip specific to their situation
- If they tag a friend: welcome the friend directly ("@[friend] — let us know when you try it 🔖")

### DMs (turn DMs into conversions)
When someone DMs asking about a recipe or the app:
1. Answer their actual question first
2. Point them to the specific recipe or feature
3. Offer the free macro calculator as a no-friction entry point
4. Don't pitch immediately — add value first

### Story engagement tools (rotate these weekly)
- Poll ("Do you meal prep?")
- Question box ("What's your go-to prep meal?")
- Quiz ("How much protein in this dish?")
- Countdown (to a new recipe drop or Sunday prep post)
- Slider ("How prepped are you for the week?" 😴 to 💪)

### Outreach (grow proactively)
Spend 15 minutes/day:
- Comment on 5 posts in #mealprep #highprotein #batchcooking with genuine, specific comments (not "🔥🔥🔥")
- Example: "The portioning system here is exactly right — most people skip this step and that's why they fail mid-week."
- Follow accounts who follow competitors with 1k–50k followers (they're active, they're your audience)
- Story mentions of anyone who shares their meal prep — tag them back

## SECTION 10 — GROWTH TACTICS

### Short-term (first 90 days)
1. **Collab Reels** — find 3 gym/fitness creators in Qatar or MENA with 5k–50k followers. Offer to co-create one Reel (you bring the food angle, they bring the training angle). Tag each other.
2. **Save-bait posts** — at least 1 post per week should be explicitly designed to be saved ("save this for Sunday"). Saves are the growth signal Instagram rewards most.
3. **Trending audio every time** — check Instagram Trending audio tab weekly. Never post without trending audio on Reels.
4. **Send 10 DMs per week** to relevant fitness accounts offering a free access code in exchange for a story mention.

### Medium-term (3–6 months)
5. **UGC (User-Generated Content)** — once you have 50+ active users, invite them to share their prep photos tagged @soulgainz.app. Repost with credit. Social proof compounds.
6. **"Try the calculator" as a story CTA** — the free macro calculator is the best lead magnet. Every week run a Story sequence ending with "your targets, calculated in 60 seconds. Try it free at soulgainz.app."
7. **Cross-post to TikTok** — same Reels, same captions. TikTok algorithm is more generous for new accounts. It costs zero to cross-post.
8. **SEO in bios** — Instagram is now an indexable search engine. Put "meal prep recipes" and "high protein meal prep" in your bio and captions consistently.

### Long-term (6–12 months)
9. **Paid promotion** — once you have 5–10 top-performing Reels, boost the best ones to MENA audiences (gym interests, 18–35, both sexes). Start at $5/day for 7 days to test.
10. **Partnership with gym brands in Qatar** — local supplement or protein brands looking for content creators. Paid or contra deals.
11. **Email list from Instagram** — every 10th caption should direct to "get the free meal plan guide → soulgainz.app." Build email subscribers you own, not followers you rent.
